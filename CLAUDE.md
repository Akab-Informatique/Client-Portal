Portal for AKAB Informatique (MSP, Lanaudière): internal team first, clients next.
Brand follows akab.ca: ink `#0a0a0b`, paper `#f4f4f1`, gold gradient
`#ffe24a → #ffce1a → #f59410`, Bricolage Grotesque (display) + Instrument Sans
(body). Logo: `src/assets/akab-logo.webp` through `src/components/brand/Logo.tsx`
(`Logo` full, `LogoMark` bars + cursor, `Wordmark` lettering). The word AKAB as a
brand mark is always the `Wordmark` image, never typed in a font. Dark theme by default.
Any UI or design work (new page, section, component, restyle): load the
`design-taste-frontend` skill first and follow it. French by default, every text in `src/i18n/fr.ts`
**and** `src/i18n/en.ts`.

## Branches

- `master` is production. Work on `feat/...` / `fix/...` branches, merge when `npm run build` and `npm test` pass.
- `master` is the only branch on GitHub; no other branches or tags are kept.

## Commands

- `npm run dev` — web :5173 + API :3001 (PGlite in `.data/`, delete it to reset)
- `npm test` — API tests (vitest, in-memory PGlite). Add a test for every new route/permission.
- `npm run build` — type-check (web + server) and bundle. Must pass before committing.
- `npm run db:generate` — after changing `server/db/schema.ts`, generates a migration in `drizzle/` (commit it). Migrations run at startup.

## Adding a module

1. `shared/permissions.ts`: add the module and its rights (labels FR/EN). The Administrator role gets every staff right automatically.
2. API: new file in `server/routes/`, mount it in `server/app.ts`, guard every route with `requirePermission("<key>")` (and `requireStaff` for staff-only areas).
3. UI: page in `src/pages/`, entry in `src/modules/registry.ts` (remove `upcoming`), route in `src/App.tsx`.
4. Tests in `tests/`.

## Weekly meeting (Rencontre hebdomadaire)

Administration tab `/meetings`, used live during the Monday team meeting (lists refresh every 10–30 s).

- Rights: `meetings.view` (see the page) and `meetings.manage` (change anything on it). Administrators only by default.
- API: `server/routes/meetings.ts`, mounted at `/api/meetings`. UI: `src/pages/meetings/` (`MeetingsPage`, `Points`, `Projects`, `Absences`).
- Sections, top to bottom:
  - **Points à discuter** (`meeting_points`, half width): a fast text field; checked off when settled; settled ones stay visible 7 days. A click opens the point's notes (`meeting_point_notes`, oldest first, `/points/:id/notes`). A point can be put off (`snooze_until`, YYYY-MM-DD Québec, after today: off the list until that day; `GET /points/later`, "Reportés (n)" to bring one back) or archived (`archived_at`; `GET /points/archive`, the "Archives" toggle of the same card, with a search; restore = back on the list). Migration `0045`. Beside it, **La semaine** (`GET /week?from=YYYY-MM-DD`, `Week.tsx`): Monday–Sunday; a day shows the team planning blocks (as `asSeen`/`seesFull` allow), absences, open tickets due and projects due that day, and Québec holidays. Events (`meeting_events`, migrations 0041–0042: date, optional HH:MM start/end, title, note, optional person, optional ticket — only its id is taken from the browser, number/title read from the ticket source) and absences are added/edited from the selected day (`/events` POST/PUT/DELETE, `meetings.manage`).
  - **Billets en retard et à échéance cette semaine** (`GET /overdue`): open tickets from the ticket source (the portal's database, or sample data locally) past due, or due by the end of Friday in Québec time (`fridayEnd` / `fridayDate`; on a weekend, the coming Friday). Shown as two gauges (overdue / due by Friday), green → red at 25, critical at 35; under each, every technician (and the unassigned tickets) as a chip in the same colors — picking one shows their count on the gauge. A third gauge (`stray` in `/overdue`): open tickets with no technician whose status is past "new" (any due date), red at 5, critical at 10, chips per status. Clicking a gauge opens a short list of its tickets (for the person picked, if any); clicking the list opens Tickets with the same filter: overdue `view=due&due=overdue`, this week `view=all&statusKind=<open kinds>&due=week` (portal filter: not overdue, due by `fridayEnd`), stray `view=unassigned&statusKind=<open kinds but new>`, plus `assignee=<id|none>`. The three gauges count only some ticket queues: by default every queue whose name does not contain "alert" (monitoring alerts), or the ones a `meetings.manage` user picked (`PUT /gauge-queues`, stored in `integrations` provider `meetings`, `gaugeQueues`; `null` = default); their links add `queue=`. Monitoring alerts (`isAlert` in `server/routes/tickets.ts`: alert queue, or a source containing alert/monitor) are left out of those gauges, their links (`alerts=0`) and La semaine; a fourth gauge lists the open alerts (newest first, red at 10, critical at 20): a click opens the ticket over the meeting (ticket workspace `ws.open`, closing it comes back to the refreshed meeting; La semaine ticket links do the same), "Résolu" sets `completeStatus` through `PATCH /api/tickets/:id` (`tickets.edit`); Tickets filter `alerts=1` shows only them.
  - **Projets en cours** (`meeting_projects`, `meeting_project_members`, `meeting_project_notes`): client (free text), due date (YYYY-MM-DD), team members (active staff only), notes newest first. Done projects are hidden behind "Terminés".
  - Absences (`meeting_absences`): one row per person and date range (both days included), kind `vacation | sick | training | personal | other`. No month calendar any more: they show and are added in **La semaine** at the top (`AbsenceDialog` in `Absences.tsx`).
  - **Tendances des billets** (under the projects, `Trends.tsx`, `GET /trends?weeks=4..52`): week by week (Monday, Québec) created / resolved / net / open at the end of the week / average and median resolution time / resolved within 24 h, from `TicketSource.history(since)` (light rows: created and completed dates; sample data seeded by the day), same queues as the gauges, no monitoring alerts. Open at a past moment = open now − came in since and still open + already there and resolved since. The last 7 days are compared with the same 7 days 1…N weeks back (slider, quick chips, or a click on a week in the chart or table).
  - **Projets en cours** will be linked to the sidebar **Projets** section (upcoming module `projects`, own sidebar section) once it is built.
- **The week ahead** (top of the page, `src/pages/meetings/WeekBrief.tsx`; `GET /api/meetings/brief?from=` (`meetings.view`), `POST /brief` (`meetings.manage`, 10 an hour) regenerates; `server/lib/weekBrief.ts`, table `meeting_week_briefs` (one per Monday), migration `0082`, AI feature `meetings.week`): 5–8 short bullets on what the week looks like and 3–5 management suggestions. `gatherFacts` reads the same data as this page (absences, team events, projects due and late, tickets due / late / unassigned by the gauges' queues, alerts) plus each technician's week as My day works it out (`dayItems` up to Friday — what is due or late and pinned —, capacity from the work hours less absences and Outlook events); the AI writes from a compact digest (`digest`), the portal's rules (`rulesBrief`: overloaded and free people, unassigned tickets, absences, late work) write it when the AI is off or fails. The week is this one Monday–Friday, the next one over a weekend (`briefWeek`). The portal's own brief is rebuilt on each read, an AI one is kept until regenerated; **each Monday** the morning run (`pipelineTick`) also writes it with the AI.
- Absences are shared with the Tickets planning calendar: `GET /api/planning/absences` (same access rules as `/planning/calendar`) hatches absent days in `WeekCalendar`, and creating or moving a block on an absent day returns the `absent` warning (still saved).
- Migrations `0023`–`0026`. Tests: `tests/api.test.ts` ("weekly meeting: …") and `tests/meetings.test.ts` (Friday cut-off).

## Pause café (coffee break)

Team › Pause café (`/breakroom`, right `breakroom.use`, technicians by default; API `server/routes/breakroom.ts`, UI `src/pages/breakroom/BreakroomPage.tsx`).
- **30 minutes a day** (Québec day): the page posts `/beat` every 30 s while it is visible; the time since the previous beat counts when it is a minute or less (`breakroom_usage`, migration 0053). Once used, every route but `/status` and `/beat` answers 403 `break_over` and the page shows "La pause est terminée" until tomorrow.
- **Échecs** (chess, `chess.js` on both sides): colleagues online = staff with a session active in the last 3 min (`GET /online`). Invite (`POST /chess`, notification `breakroom.invite` → `/breakroom?game=<id>`; one open game per pair), the invited one accepts or declines (`/respond`); the one who invited plays white. Every move is checked on the server (`/move` `{ from, to, promotion? }`; the game is replayed from its PGN so repetition counts; a concurrent change answers `game_changed`); ends on checkmate, stalemate, draw or `/resign` (which also cancels an unanswered invitation). `chess_games` (FEN + PGN, migration 0053; the earlier checkers table is dropped in 0052). The board polls every 1.5 s. Tests: `tests/api.test.ts` ("coffee break").
- **Complots** (Rikki Tahta's bluffing game, Ferti; French Coup): `server/lib/complots.ts` is the rules engine (pure functions on a JSON state: `newGame`, `play`, `tick` for timeouts — answer 15 s, turn 90 s, choices 30–45 s —, `leave`, `viewFor` hides other players' cards); tests `tests/complots.test.ts`. Tables `complots_games` (migration 0056: host, `seats` with answers, the 5 `chars`, `state`, `rev` for concurrent writes; one change at a time per table in-process). Routes `/api/breakroom/complots` (list, create + `breakroom.complots` notification, `/respond`, `/start` by the host with 2–6 who said yes, `/play`, `/leave`). UI `src/pages/breakroom/Complots.tsx` (custom cards in the clans' colours). One character per clan: Complots' Duchesse, Assassin, Comtesse, Capitaine, Ambassadeur / Inquisiteur; from Complots 2 only the Bourreau and the Justicier (their powers are in its rules; the other 7 cards' texts are not).
- **Orbi l'extraterrestre**: the channel's public feed (`GET /videos`: YouTube RSS of channel `UCWwdio93JMiS987S0lo6RXg`, read by the server, kept 30 min, last list kept on failure), played in a popup through `youtube-nocookie.com` (CSP `frame-src`; thumbnails from `i.ytimg.com` in `img-src`).

## Heures payées, RH et temps facturable (Feuille de temps, Gestion › RH)

**What an employee sees** (the Timesheet page, the profile report, their own PDF `GET /time/week/pdf`): **hours only** — what they entered by type and day by day, the work day, the hours to pay — **no money, no wage, no vacation accrual, no balances**. Amounts, the 4 % and the balances stay in the complete report that accounting receives and that managers / admins see (`seesMoney`: those rights, **only when looking at someone else's timesheet — one's own is always the hours-only copy, whatever the role**).

**Replaces** the older notes above on the work day "not counted", the hours bank switch / profile default (`banked`, `bankBalanceX100`, `bank_insufficient`), billable ratio on screens, and "an owner corrects a sent entry". Migration `0115`, `server/lib/hr.ts`, `server/routes/hr.ts` (`/api/hr`), `src/pages/hr/HrPage.tsx`, `src/components/HrFile.tsx`; tests "timesheet: paid hours, the RH file…", "a time entry changed on its ticket".
- **Three kinds of hours, never mixed.** (1) **Paid hours** (what the employee is paid; the only hours the timesheet screen, the profile report, the PDF and the email show): work days (start–end less lunch, `shared/workday.ts`) **less the hours put in the bank**, plus Vacances, Maladie, Férié and Paiement de banque d'heures (`weekPay`). Training, meetings, trips and ticket time are *inside* the work day: they add no pay. (2) **Billable** and (3) **non billable** hours are only **stored per client** (`timesheet_entries.client_id`, `billable_x100`, `non_billable_x100`; ticket entries take the ticket's client, project time the project's, a trip the chosen one) and shown nowhere; `clientHourTotals` reads them, `productivityPct(billable, workday)` = billable ÷ work-day hours (7,5 h worked, 9 h billed = 120 %). Not billable = hours − billable, never below 0 (`splitBillable`).
- **Manual types** (the only ones offered): Journée de travail (`workday`), Paiement de banque d'heures (`bankpay`), Formation, Réunion (`other`), Déplacement (`travel`), Vacances (`vacation`), Maladie (`sick`), Férié (`holiday`); ticket time is `labour` and comes from tickets only. The built-in kinds are always there (`BUILT_IN_KINDS`, `ensureTypes`); "Administration" is turned off by the migration. Work day: `bankHours` (≤ the day) are put in the bank and **not paid that week**. Holiday: no hours typed — `holidayPay` = 1/20 of the wages of the 4 complete weeks before the holiday's week (work days less banked, vacation and sick hours, each week capped at the overtime threshold — 40 h by default — × the hourly wage), stored in `amount_cents`, hours = amount ÷ wage. Trip: hours, optional ticket (client taken from it) or client, **non billable by default**; ticked billable it needs the amount ($) and a client (`travel_amount_required`, `travel_client_required`), its hours count billable and it becomes one `charge` line `ptravel:<entry>` in Approbation (never auto-approved except by the contract's rule; locked once approved).
- **RH file** (`hr_profiles`: hourly wage, overtime threshold; `hr_ledger`: every move of three accounts — **hours bank in $**, **vacation bank in $**, **sick days in hours**). Right **`hr.manage`** (administrators only: the RH page in Gestion, every employee, edit everything). **An employee never sees any RH data, not even their own** (no RH card in the profile; the `hr.own` right was removed). The RH tab is also in the admin user sheet (`hr.manage`) and opens in a window from the RH page. Entries move the accounts by themselves (`applyEntryLedger`, rows tied to the entry go with it): bank hours add (hours × wage), a payout takes off, vacation takes hours × wage, sick takes hours. Needing the wage and missing it answers `hr_wage_missing`. **Below zero is allowed**: the entry is kept, the answer says `negative`, every `hr.manage` user gets a `hr.negative` notification and the PDF flags it. A hand change (`POST /hr/:id/adjust`, hours or $, + or −) needs `confirm: true` (the tick in the screen) and is audited. **Each week sent adds 4 % of its gross pay** (work days paid, sick, holiday, bank payouts; not vacation taken) to the vacation bank (`refreshAccrual`, one row a week, recalculated when a manager changes a sent week, removed when it is open again). Balances already banked before this change are **not** carried over: HR enters the starting balances by hand.
- **Who can change what.** A technician adds, changes and removes their entries **only until the week is sent** (`timesheet_locked` after); a manager (`timesheets.manage`) any time. Ticket time can be changed on its ticket (`PATCH /api/tickets/:id/time/:entryId`, pencil in the activity, `EditTime.tsx`): hours, **billed hours (may exceed the hours worked**: `ticket_time_entries.billed_minutes`, "Facturé" box of the time entry window, `billedHours`), billable, notes — its owner until the week is sent, **never once its work to bill is approved or invoiced** (`entry_billed`); the timesheet row follows (`timesheet_entries.ticket_time_id`). Billable time is never auto-approved: it goes to Approbation, then the contract's auto-approve rule applies as before.
- The PDF/email for accounting (`timesheetModel`): hours paid by type with amounts, the 4 % accrued, the three balances, day by day.

ITCloud's partner API (`https://zone.itcloud.ca/api/partner`, docs behind the partner login): every call carries `Accept-Version: 1` and a **JWT signed RS256 with the partner's RSA private key** (`signToken`: `iss` = the integration key GUID, `aud` = the partner API, 10 minutes, scopes `customer.read product.read report.read`). Keys: integration key in `integrations.config`, private key PEM **sealed** in `integrations.secret`, never sent back (`hasKey`); only GET calls (customers, products, services-report; the report is limited to 30 a minute), **nothing is ever written to ITCloud**. Switching it on makes the vendor **ITCloud** in Admin › PSA › Fournisseurs (`ensureVendor`).
- **1. Customers ↔ clients** (`itcloud_clients`: a customer has one client, a client may have several customers; `GET|PUT /clients`): proposed by the same name (accents, case, punctuation, inc/ltée… ignored) or the same email (client, billing email or a contact), only when unambiguous; applied only once accepted (one by one, or "accept all"), removable.
- **2. Products → services** (`GET|POST /services/plan`): each **recurring, not usage-based** ITCloud product becomes a portal service (Inventory › Services) with `sku = ITC-<productId>`, `vendor_id` = ITCloud, `vendor_sku` = the product id (`services` columns `sku`, `vendor_id`, `vendor_sku`, unique); monthly or yearly from its cycles; **a new service is made with no price and no cost (0): they are typed by hand in Inventory › Services and ITCloud never sets or changes them** on their own. **On demand only**, the button **"Synchroniser les prix depuis ITCloud"** (`POST /api/admin/itcloud/services/prices { applyToContracts }`, `resyncServicePrices`, audited `itcloud.prices_resynced`) puts on every ITCloud service the **cost = ITCloud's unit price** (the product's `monthlyUnitPrice` / `annuallyUnitPrice`) and the **price = ITCloud's list price** (MSRP from the invoice items report, `msrpByProduct`; a product with no list price keeps its price, nothing is invented), asks whether the ITCloud lines of running contracts follow or only the services (future additions), and says the **margin it comes out to** (average, how many are under 15 %; the review list also marks each row's margin, red at 0 or less). A one-time button under the review list, **"Remettre à zéro les prix et coûts des services ITCloud"** (`POST /api/admin/itcloud/services/zero`, `admin.integrations`, `zeroServiceMoney`, audited `itcloud.services_zeroed`), puts every ITCloud service and its contract lines back to 0 $. Without a contract yet, a matched service just shows "Aucun contrat actif" (nothing is created, no error) and is picked up by the next sync once the client has one. Reviewed first (new / update / same), applied for the ticked ones; a service with the same name and no vendor is tied instead of duplicated.
- **3. Sync** (`POST /sync`, `syncItcloud`): the services report is kept in `itcloud_services` (with each row's state: `synced`, `client_unlinked`, `product_unmatched`, `no_contract`, `inactive`); every **Active** service of a matched customer whose product (matched by group + name to its product id, else by name when unique) is a portal service becomes a line of the client's contract (`contract_services` with `quantity`, `source = itcloud`, `external_id` = ITCloud service id; unit price and unit cost = **the portal service's, typed by hand, copied when the line is made** (the sync never overwrites them afterwards; they change only through the questions of "Service prices" below), start day = **the contract's start day** (never ITCloud's registration date; an existing line is moved to it at the next sync); a service added by hand also starts on the contract's start day unless another day is picked). **There is no automatic contract**: a service waits **unmapped** (state `no_contract`, "À associer à un contrat") until it is mapped to one of the client's active contracts — by **customer** in step 1 (a select on each matched row, `itcloud_clients.contract_id`, migration `0095`, `PUT /clients/:id/contract`: all that customer's services land on it at the next sync), by **service** in step 3 (a select on each active row, `itcloud_service_contracts`, migration `0096`, `PUT /services/:id/contract`; wins over the customer's; the line is made or moved at once from the report kept at the last sync, `setServiceContract`; "Non associé" removes the line again), or **from the client's contract** (Contracts tab › services › **"Associer un service ITCloud (n)"**, `GET|POST /api/crm/clients/:id/contracts/:kid/itcloud-services`, `crm.billing`: the client's unmapped, active ITCloud services whose product is a portal service, ticked and put on that contract at once, `unmappedServices`; `itcloud_services.portal_service_id`, migration `0097`). A line already on a contract stays while its service is active, even if no pick covers it any more. A later sync updates quantity / cost / contract; a service no longer active (or no longer matched) at ITCloud **leaves the contract**; a vendor line cannot be removed by hand (`service_from_vendor`) and shows an ITCloud badge and "× quantity" in the Contracts tab. The prorata of a line is worked out on price × quantity. Nothing bills these amounts yet (like the other contract services).
- **Billed directly by ITCloud** (`itcloud_clients.direct_billed`, migration `0125`; tick "Facturé directement par ITCloud" on a matched customer in step 1, `PUT /api/admin/itcloud/clients/:id/direct { directBilled }`, `setCustomerDirect`, audited `itcloud.direct_billed`): ITCloud bills that customer itself, so there is nothing to charge here. Its services are **never put on a contract** — the sync marks them `direct_billed` (a state of step 3), the lines they already had leave the contract at the next sync (like a service that ended), per-service contract picks are cleared, and mapping one by hand answers `customer_direct_billed`. Unticking it lets the next sync take them up like any customer. Test: "ITCloud: vendor, clients matched…" (end).

Steps 1 (customers) and 2 (products) have a selection — the rows that can be picked start picked — with **Tout sélectionner / Sélectionner la page / Désélectionner la page / Tout désélectionner**; only the ticked rows **of the filtered list** are accepted / applied (the count is on the button). ## Service prices and the contracts that use them

A price (or cost) is never moved on other contracts without asking (`server/lib/servicePrices.ts`, `src/components/ScopeDialog.tsx`; test "a price changed on a service, or on a contract line, asks how far it goes"). "Running contracts" = active ones.
- **Changing a service's price or cost** (Inventory › Services, `PATCH /api/services/:id`, `services.manage`): when it really changes and the service is on lines of running contracts, the server answers 409 `price_scope_required` (`fields: [{ path: "contracts", message: "<how many>" }]`) and the page asks: **yes, also in those contracts** (`applyToContracts: true`: every line of the service in a running contract gets the new price / cost, one "service changed" event on each, monthly figures re-worked) or **no, only the service, for future additions** (`false`; the contracts keep theirs). A change of anything else asks nothing.
- **Changing a price on a contract line** (the line editor of the contract window, `PATCH /clients/:id/contracts/:kid/services/:sid`, `crm.billing`; **ITCloud lines too — their price can be edited, their quantity and day stay the vendor's**, `service_from_vendor`): a new price asks first (409 `price_scope_required`, `message` = how many OTHER running contracts have the service) with three ways: **only this contract** (`scope: "line"`), **this contract and the service's price, for future additions** (`"service"`) or **this contract, the service and the other running contracts** (`"all"`, offered only when there are others). A quantity or day change asks nothing.

## Paiements fournisseurs (Comptabilité › Paiements fournisseurs)

`/accounting/vendor-payments` (right `accounting.view`; `accounting.manage` for the refresh button; API `server/routes/vendorPayments.ts` at `/api/vendor-payments`, library `server/lib/vendorBills.ts`, UI `src/pages/accounting/VendorPaymentsPage.tsx`, table `vendor_bills`, migration `0120`, test "vendor payments: …" in `tests/api.test.ts`).
- **Read only from QuickBooks**: `pullBills` copies the QuickBooks **Bills** (vendor, number, issue and due day, total, **balance** = what is left to pay, in cents) into `vendor_bills`, found by `quickbooks_id`; nothing is written to QuickBooks. Each pass reads the open bills (`Balance > 0`), the ones changed since the last pass (`imports.bills.at`; a year back the first time) and looks up one by one the open bills we hold that QuickBooks no longer lists: paid → balance 0, gone → removed here. The vendor is tied to the portal vendor with the same `quickbooks_id` (Admin › PSA › Fournisseurs) when there is one, else only the name is kept.
- Runs every 15 minutes in `quickbooksTick` when the sync switch **"Factures fournisseurs"** (`sync.bills`, on by default, Admin › Intégrations › QuickBooks) is on, and from the page's "Rafraîchir depuis QuickBooks" (`POST /sync`, 409 `quickbooks_not_connected`, 502 `quickbooks_failed`; audited `vendor_bills.synced`).

- Page: four tiles, a month calendar (Monday first; each day shows the total to pay and the number of bills, red when the day has passed, a click lists the day's bills), the late bills and the ones with no due date.
- **Prévision de trésorerie** (under the calendar, `VendorForecast.tsx`, `server/lib/cashForecast.ts`; `GET /forecast?days=14..180` (60 by default), `PUT /forecast/settings` (`accounting.manage`): `balance` in dollars, `payDelayDays`, `lateDelayDays`; stored in `integrations` provider `vendor.forecast`): day by day from the **bank balance typed by hand** (the portal does not read the bank; the date it was typed is shown). **In**: the portal's invoices not voided and not paid, what is left after payments, on the due day + `payDelayDays` (clients' usual delay, 0 by default); one already late counts as collected `lateDelayDays` (14) from today. **Out**: the unpaid vendor bills of `vendor_bills`, on their due day, a late one paid today. Invoices and bills with no due date are left off the line and totalled apart (`leftOut`). The answer has `series` (balance per day), `events`, `lowest` and `shortfall` (the first day the balance goes under zero and by how much — the verdict banner "On peut tout payer" / "Il manquera de l'argent dès le …"). A forecast only: nothing is booked, paid or collected. Test: "forecasts the cash…".
- **Tabs** (`?tab=calendar|forecast|recurring|payroll`): Calendrier, Prévision, Paiements récurrents, Paie (only with `hr.manage`).

## Security rules

- Authorization is enforced in the API, never only in the UI. Use `auth(c)` / `requirePermission`; never trust ids or roles sent by the browser.
- Mutations: JSON only + same-origin check (`sameOriginJson`). Sessions are server-side (`sessions` table, cookie holds a random token; DB stores its SHA-256).
- Passwords: scrypt (`server/lib/crypto.ts`), policy ≥ 12 chars. MFA (TOTP) required in production (off by default in dev, `REQUIRE_MFA=true` to test); codes are single-use. MFA secrets sealed with `APP_SECRET`.
- **Signing in with a push notification on the phone** (migration `0091`; `server/lib/pushAuth.ts`, `server/routes/pushAuth.ts` at `/api/auth/push`; UI `src/lib/push.ts`, `PhoneApproval` in `LoginPage.tsx`, `src/pages/ApprovePage.tsx` at `/approve`, `src/components/PhoneSignInCard.tsx` in the profile; `public/sw.js` + `manifest.webmanifest`; packages `web-push`, `@simplewebauthn/server|browser`): a way to satisfy the second step instead of the authenticator code (which always stays as the fallback; it only applies where MFA is required, `REQUIRE_MFA`). **Enrolling** (a fully signed-in session, **the password asked again**, rate limited, not in "view as client"): the phone makes a **passkey** (WebAuthn, platform authenticator, `userVerification: required`: the private key stays in the phone's secure hardware and is unlocked by its fingerprint / face / PIN), subscribes to web push and gets a random **device token** (only its SHA-256 is kept; the push subscription is sealed with `APP_SECRET`); table `push_devices`; a bell alert `security.alert` tells the person. **Signing in**: after the password the session exists but is not allowed anything; `POST /start` makes a `push_challenges` row **bound to that session** (single use, 2 minutes, 6 an hour per person, 15 s apart) with a random nonce and a **number shown on the computer**; the push carries no secret (only the request id); the phone (`/approve`, no session) proves itself with its device token and shows where the request comes from (browser / OS, IP, time) and **three numbers** (number matching: approving by reflex fails; two wrong numbers close the request); the passkey signs the nonce (verified against the stored public key, origin and RP id, user verified, signature counter) and only then `markSessionMfaVerified` unlocks **that one session** — an approval never unlocks another browser. **"Ce n'est pas moi"** closes that session, rests the account 15 minutes and tells the administrators (`notifyUserLocked`) and the person. **Push endpoints are checked** (`pushEndpointOk`: https, no port or credentials, and only the browsers' push services — googleapis.com, push.services.mozilla.com, push.apple.com, notify.windows.com — so a subscription can never make the portal send a request to an address of the person's choosing); enrolling needs HTTPS in production. The waiting browser looks at `GET /auth/push/status` every second through one chain of timeouts (and at once when the tab comes back to the screen) — never in an effect that depends on a function recreated at each render (that stopped the check from ever running). **When a push cannot be sent the reason is kept** (`pushDetailed` returns the push service's status and a few harmless words: `refused` 401/403 (e.g. `BadJwtToken` = the VAPID keys of the server are not the ones the phone subscribed with), `gone` 404/410 (the phone is removed), `throttled` 429, `service` 5xx, `network` (no answer: the server cannot reach the push service over HTTPS), `bad_subscription`): it is in the server log (`[push] send to "…" failed`), in the audit event `push.challenge_sent` (`failures`), on the sign-in page under the error, and in the answer of the profile's Tester button. A sign-in is limited to 10 requests an hour per person, 15 s apart; the page names the limit when it is hit. Phones can be removed from the profile (or are removed when an administrator resets the person's MFA, or when the push service says the subscription is gone); everything is audited (`push.*`). Web push keys (VAPID) are made once and kept sealed in `integrations` provider `push`; the RP id / origin come from `PUBLIC_URL` (HTTPS in production; the server must reach the phones' push services over HTTPS); optional `PUSH_CONTACT` is the VAPID contact email. **iPhone / iPad**: notifications only work for the portal added to the home screen (the profile says so). Tests: `tests/api.test.ts` ("sign-in approved on the phone"), with a software authenticator (real WebAuthn registration and assertions) and web push mocked.
- **The portal's notifications on the phone** (migration `0092`; `server/lib/pushNotify.ts`): the phones enrolled for sign-in also get the bell's notifications as web push — `notify()` (`server/lib/notify.ts`) hands each batch to `pushOut` after saving it, never waiting and never failing. Per phone a switch `push_devices.notify` (on at enrolment; `PATCH /api/auth/push/devices/:id { notify }`, own phones only) and a test button (`POST /devices/:id/test`, 6 per 5 min); per person `user_preferences.push_details` (**off by default**: the lock screen only says the kind — "Nouveau texto", "Tâche pour vous" — and "Touchez pour ouvrir le portail"; on adds the ticket number / title / text, `pushText`), set in the profile (`PUT /api/me/preferences { pushDetails }`). The payload is encrypted end to end (RFC 8291, the push service only carries it), carries a **path of the portal** only (`safePath`: never another site; the service worker checks it again), at most 30 pushes per person per 10 minutes, and a phone the push service says is gone is removed. Tests: "the portal's notifications reach the enrolled phone".
- Admin changes are written to `audit_log` via `audit()`. Never log secrets.

## Hours bank contracts (latest)

A contract of kind **banked** ("Banque d'heures") sells hours of work by the month. **No service to pick**; the new-contract form asks **hours per month**, **price per hour** and what becomes of the hours not used in the month (`client_contracts.bank_rate_cents`, `bank_rollover`, migration `0126`; `bankedHours`, `bankedRate`, `bankRollover` on `POST` / `PATCH /api/crm/clients/:id/contracts`; always charged monthly). Rollover (`shared/bank.ts`, `bankMonths`, calendar months in Québec time): **lose** (every month starts with the bank), **carry** (unused hours add to next month, the full bank is still bought) or **topup** (unused hours stay, next month only buys what fills the bank — `contractBills` makes that month's line for the top-up hours only, none when nothing is needed). ## Procédures (SOP) (My tools › Procédures)

Step-by-step guides made by recording a screen (`/sops`, `?sop=<id>` opens one; rights `sops.view` / `sops.edit` (technicians by default) / `sops.manage` (administrators: other people's drafts, change or delete anyone's); API `server/routes/sops.ts` at `/api/sops`, UI `src/pages/sops/` (`SopsPage`, `SopEditor`, `RecorderDialog`, `StepImage`, `SopDocument`), browser engine `src/lib/sop/` (`recorder.ts`, `diff.ts`, `image.ts`), tables `sops` + `sop_steps` (migration `0129`); tests "procedures (SOP)" in `tests/api.test.ts`, `tests/sopDiff.test.ts`).
- **What a web page can and cannot do** (decides the design): it can record any screen, window or browser tab, **including other applications** (`getDisplayMedia`, the browser's own picker; nothing is captured without it), but it **cannot see mouse clicks made outside its own page**. So a step is **a picture of the screen at a chosen moment**, and the pin (where to click) is placed afterwards in the editor. Real click tracking of other applications would need a native helper or a browser extension (not built).
- **Recording** (`ScreenRecorder` in `src/lib/sop/recorder.ts`, started from `RecorderDialog`: new procedure — asks for a name — or more steps for one that exists): steps are taken **by hand** (the button of the dialog, or of the floating window, or Space in it) and/or **automatically** when the screen changes and settles (`ChangeWatcher` in `diff.ts`: the frame shrunk to a 64×36 grey grid, a step when ≥ 2 % of the cells differ from the last step and the picture stopped moving for 0.9 s, never twice within 1.5 s; a moving mouse or a blinking cursor never counts — pure functions, tested). The frame comes from `ImageCapture.grabFrame()` (a hidden `<video>` is the fallback), looked at every 0.5 s. **Floating window** (`openFloating`: Document Picture-in-Picture, Chrome / Edge; plain DOM with its own styles; capture, Auto, pause, finish; it also drives the timers so a page in the background is not slowed down by the browser): it needs a click, so it is tried right after the picker and a button opens it otherwise (a failure from that button is told). Sharing **a window or a tab** keeps the floating window off the pictures; sharing the whole screen shows it (the dialog warns). Optional **video** (`MediaRecorder`, webm): only offered for download at the end, **never uploaded**. Nothing leaves the browser until "Terminer"; then the procedure is made and the steps are sent one by one (a failure can be retried without sending a step twice). Closing the page while recording asks to confirm.
- **Pictures** (`image.ts`): every picture is re-encoded in the browser as a JPEG of at most 1920 px wide before it is sent (a 4K screenshot would be several MB as PNG); the server accepts JPEG / PNG up to 4 MB, checks the **bytes** against the type (`bad_image`) and serves them with the type it knows and `nosniff` (`GET /:id/steps/:sid/image`, base64 in `sop_steps.image`). Other ways to add a step: paste (Windows + Maj + S then Ctrl + V works for anything on screen — the off-browser fallback), drop or choose image files, or a text-only step.
- **Editor**: title and description (✨ Corriger for those with `tickets.ai`), a card per step (title, words, picture, move up / down, add a text step after, delete) saved when a field is left. Tools over the picture: **Épingle** (a numbered pin where to click), **Cadre** (a frame), **Gomme**, and **Flouter** — drag over a password or a client's name and the area is **pixelated in the picture itself** (`pixelate`: the original pixels are not kept). Pins and frames are `marks` (fractions 0–1 of the picture: `{ kind: "pin" | "box", x, y, w?, h? }`, 40 at most), drawn over the picture so they look the same in the editor, the reader, the print and the file.
- **Reading and sharing**: a procedure is a **draft** (its author's, plus managers) until published (`sop_empty` when it has no step); published ones are read by everyone with `sops.view`; only the author or `sops.manage` changes or deletes (`forbidden`). Whoever may only read gets the white sheet (`SopSheet`) with **Imprimer / PDF** (`SopPrint`, `html.printing-sop` in `index.css`, same idea as the quote) and **Télécharger (HTML)** (`buildHtml`: one self-contained file with the pictures inside, to email or open anywhere). 150 steps at most (`too_many_steps`). Audited `sop.created` / `published` / `unpublished` / `deleted`.
- **Automatic video edit** (`src/lib/sop/montage.ts`, on the "saved" screen of `RecorderDialog` when a video was kept: **Créer la vidéo montée**): the recording is played into a canvas and the canvas is filmed again (MediaRecorder, webm VP9, 4 Mbit/s, the recording's own size) as: the AKAB logo on black with the procedure's name (2.6 s), a cross-fade into the recording, a cross-fade out, then the logo, a gold "Merci !" / "Thank you!" (language of the portal) and akab.ca (3.6 s). Done on this computer, never uploaded; **it lasts as long as the recording** (progress bar, cancel), and the tab should stay in front. The beat comes from a worker (`public/ticker.js`, same origin so the CSP allows it) because a page timer drops to once a second in a background tab and the video came out choppy; a page timer is the fallback. No audio. The original recording stays downloadable too. Not tried on a long real recording.
- **Branding of the files**: the reading sheet / print (`SopSheet`) and the downloaded HTML (`buildHtml`) follow akab.ca: an ink band with the logo in its black pill (`akab-logo-pill.png`), the gold gradient rule, Bricolage Grotesque for titles and Instrument Sans for the text (**the HTML file carries the fonts and the logo inside it** — woff2 imported with `?inline`, about 75 KB — so it looks the same on any computer), gold step numbers on ink, and an ink footer with akab.ca. Backgrounds are forced to print (`print-color-adjust: exact`). Pictures are the screenshots only; the video edit above follows the same colours.
- Not built: AI that writes each step's title from its picture (the AI here is text only), click tracking outside the page (see above), step videos kept on the server, links from tickets or the knowledge base. Not tried in a real Chrome with a real screen share: the engine was run end to end against a fake screen source (automatic capture, by-hand steps, video, save, editor, blur, print, file), and the floating window could not open in the test browser.

## API keys for outside tools (Grok…) (latest)

`Authorization: Bearer akab_…` on any `/api/*` call (`server/lib/apiKeys.ts`, table `api_keys`, migration `0168`; routes `/api/admin/api-keys`, `server/routes/apiKeys.ts`, right `admin.integrations` = master administrators; test "API keys for outside tools"). A key **acts as the person who made it** but with only the rights ticked on it (`GET /rights` lists what they may tick) and **never more than that person has today** (checked on every call); **read only unless `write` is set** (any non-GET answers 403 `api_key_read_only`). A key can never carry `admin.*`, `hr.*`, `passbolt.*`, `outlook.*`, `chat.*`, `clientview.*`, `timesheets.manage`, `crm.delete`, `tickets.delete`, and never reaches `/api/auth`, `/api/admin` (so it cannot manage keys), `/api/me` (except GET), `/api/microsoft`, `/api/passbolt`, `/api/outlook`, `/api/chat`, `/api/view-as`, `/api/pay`. Shown once at creation (`POST { name, permissions[], write, expiresInDays? }` → `key`), only its SHA-256 is kept; revoke with `DELETE /:id`; 120 calls a minute per key; `lastUsedAt` / `lastIp` kept; audited `api_key.created` / `revoked`. A bad, revoked or expired key is 401, never anonymous. Routes keep doing their own right checks, so everything the key can reach is already guarded. **Screen**: Admin › Clés d'API (`src/admin/ApiKeysSection.tsx`, menu item `apikeys` under Intégrations; name, expiry in days, write switch, rights ticked by module, the key shown once with a copy button, list with last use and revoke). **For the outside tool**: `GET /api/openapi.json` (any key or staff session; `server/lib/openapi.ts` is a hand-kept OpenAPI 3.1 list of the useful read calls and the main writes — tickets, notes, clients, projects, tasks, quotes, invoices —, loose response shapes; add a route there when a tool needs it). Give Grok the file's address and the key as a Bearer token. The screen was built and type-checked but not looked at in a browser.
