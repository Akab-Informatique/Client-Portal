Linking QuickBooks Online
to the AKAB Portal

Admin › Intégrations › QuickBooks

1Before you start

  • You are signed in as a master admin (needs the rights admin.integrations and admin.billing).
  • PUBLIC_URL in the server's .env is your real HTTPS address, for example https://portail.akab.ca.
  • Do a first pass in Sandbox. Switching between sandbox and production resets the connection.

2Create the app at Intuit

  1. Go to developer.intuit.com and sign in (create a developer account if needed).
  2. Create an app for QuickBooks Online with the Accounting scope. This is the only scope the portal asks for.
  3. On the app's Keys & OAuth page, copy the Client ID and Client Secret. Sandbox and production have separate keys.
  4. Under Redirect URIs, add exactly:
    https://<your-portal>/api/billing/quickbooks/callback
    The portal shows this address on its QuickBooks page too, so you can copy it from there.
  5. For production keys, Intuit asks you to complete its app questionnaire first.

3Connect in the portal

  1. Open Admin › Intégrations › QuickBooks.
  2. Choose the environment (Sandbox or Production).
  3. Paste the Client ID and Client Secret. The secret is stored encrypted and is never shown again.
  4. Click Connecter QuickBooks, sign in to Intuit and pick your company.
  5. You come back to the portal: "QuickBooks est connecté" with the company number. Connecting turns the sync on.

4Set it up, in this order

StepWhat to do
Clients ↔ customersThe portal suggests matches by name or email. Nothing is applied until you review and accept. An unmapped client can be created in QuickBooks instead (refused if the name already exists).
Chart of accountsReview the proposed plan, tick what you accept, apply. Matching is by id, then number, then name. QuickBooks-only accounts are added here; portal-only accounts are turned off, never deleted. Then set the default accounts in Admin › Facturation.
Items and categoriesAlso proposed first; only what you accept is applied.
VendorsTick only the QuickBooks vendors you want kept in step. A ticked vendor is tied to the portal vendor of the same name, or added if there is none.
Invoices from QuickBooksChoose where the first copy starts: from an invoice number or from a date. Check the preview (how many invoices would come). Invoices of clients you have not mapped are skipped.
Next invoice numberClick Aligner sur QuickBooks. The portal's next number is raised to follow QuickBooks' highest; it is never lowered. It is re-checked before each new invoice.
SwitchesTurn on the parts you want: customers, accounts, vendors, invoices in, invoices out, payments. Leave timesheets and expenses off for now.

5How it works day to day

  • Two-way sync every 15 minutes, on Synchroniser maintenant, and right after you edit a client, account or vendor in the portal.
  • Who wins: only the portal changed → pushed to QuickBooks. Only QuickBooks changed → pulled into the portal. Both changed → the latest wins.
  • Nothing is ever deleted; records are turned off instead.
  • Invoices made in the portal are sent to QuickBooks; sending again updates the invoice instead of doubling it. Payments come back from QuickBooks every 15 minutes.

6Check that it worked

  • The page shows Connecté and the last sync times.
  • The test button answers "QuickBooks répond".
  • Change a client's phone number in the portal; a minute later it is changed on the customer in QuickBooks.
  • If a sync fails, the error appears on that part's card.
Good to know. Menu names in Intuit's console may differ slightly from the above. The portal part follows the current code.
